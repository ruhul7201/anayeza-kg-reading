Teacher Reference · Foundation Stage Pacing

A Happy, Focused Reading Session

Each session balances teacher modelling, small-group support, independent practice and conversation. The routine is predictable enough to make children feel secure, but playful enough to keep them curious.

⏱️ Timing Blueprint — a Foundation Stage session

Modernised from the classic 4-part reading block format.

01
⭐

MINI LESSON 5–8 MIN

  • One focused skill, whole class
  • Short, playful, tied to the week's book
02
👥

SMALL GROUPS & CONFERENCES 15–20 MIN

  • Meet each Lexile-level group
  • Others rotate through independent centers
03
📖

INDEPENDENT / LEVELUP READING 15–20 MIN

  • Choice within levelled books
  • Rosen LevelUp for KG1 & KG2
04
💬

SHARE & REFLECT 5 MIN

  • Student share-outs, reflect & celebrate
  • Set a small goal for next time
🕐 Pre-KG runs a shorter 25–30 min version twice a week; KG1 & KG2 run the full ~45 min block once a week in the Reading Room, plus 20 minutes of in-class Rosen LevelUp reading.
1 · Whole class

⭐ Mini Lesson

Teach one focused skill to the whole group — short, playful and tied to that week's book.

  • Keep it short and interactive
  • Use a song, action or picture prompt
  • Connect to the class reading goal
2 · Small groups

👥 Small Groups & Conferences

Meet each Lexile-level group while others explore independently.

  • Targeted, level-based instruction
  • Differentiate by need
  • Use assessment data to form groups
3 · Independent

📖 Independent Reading

Build reading stamina and confidence with a book of their own choosing.

  • Choice within levelled books
  • Builds fluency & comprehension
  • Rosen LevelUp for KG1/KG2
4 · Whole class

💬 Share & Reflect

Bring everyone back together to celebrate what was read.

  • Student share-outs
  • Reflect on what was learned
  • Set a small goal for next time

Teaching & Learning Strategies

Fun, Phonics & Reading Activities

Young children learn to read best when reading feels meaningful, active and fun. These experiences build phonics, vocabulary, comprehension, confidence and a love of books.

All grades · Pre-reading

🖼️ Picture Walk

Learners preview the pictures before reading to predict the story, activate prior knowledge and build vocabulary. In KG2 this stretches into higher-order thinking questions.

  • “Look at the cover — what do you see? What might happen?”
  • Doubles as a formative assessment tool for teachers
  • Pre-KG visits the Reading Room twice a week for Picture Walk + Read Aloud
▶ 🎥 Read Aloud demo: “Too Much Glue” (Storyline Online)
KG1 · Phonics

🔤 Blend & Read

Phonics in action — learners blend phonemes into words and segment words back into sounds, moving from CVC words to simple sentences.

  • This week: Blend n Read — Story of Pip (slides + printable)
  • Lowercase alphabet flashcards for letter–sound recall
  • Reading stations with a poster-guided rotation
▶ 🎥 Jolly Phonics: Letter Sounds s, a, t, i, p, n
KG2 · Small groups

🦉 Guided Reading

Teacher-supported reading in levelled groups using Oxford Reading Tree books. Students meet Biff, Chip, Kipper and Floppy while building fluency and comprehension.

  • Beginner → Pets · Intermediate → At the Animal Park · Advanced → Barn Owls
  • Comprehension talk: characters, setting, fiction vs non-fiction
  • Groups are formed from Lexile levels and refreshed each term
▶ 🎥 Guided Reading: Running Small Groups in the Classroom
Whole programme

🌟 Star Reader Rewards

Effort and growth get celebrated, not just level. Our reward system keeps motivation high across the Foundation Stage.

  • Star Reader certificates for weekly reading champions
  • Top Growers & Top Readers recognised every term
  • LevelUp completion certificates for digital reading streaks
KG1 & KG2 · Digital

💻 Rosen LevelUp Reading

20 minutes of in-class digital reading weekly. KG1 reads a whole-class weekly book linked to class themes; KG2 reads differentiated books by level.

  • Baseline assessment sets each child’s starting Lexile
  • QR login badges make sign-in easy for little readers
  • Weekly assignment also shared for reading at home
Intervention

🌱 Sprout Readers Support

Lower achievers are identified early and supported with additional pull-out reading sessions focused on phonics, decoding and comprehension.

  • Small-group targeted practice
  • Progress reviewed against weekly monitoring records
  • Growth celebrated on the Top Growers list

📐 How We Measure Reading

Multiple sources of evidence · recorded throughout the year

Reading progress is assessed through a combination of standardised reading measures, teacher observation, oral reading, and competency-based assessment to provide a well-rounded picture of each child's development.

📊 Rosen LevelUp baseline & Lexile tracking

Children's reading ability is established through a Rosen LevelUp baseline, with Lexile measures used where appropriate to monitor reading growth and guide suitable reading levels and texts.

👀 Teacher observation & oral reading

Teachers regularly observe children during reading, storytelling, phonics activities, and individual oral reading to assess accuracy, fluency, vocabulary, comprehension, confidence, and expression.

🔤 Early literacy & phonological skills

Assessment includes recognition of sounds, beginning/middle/ending sounds, blending and segmenting, familiar word recognition, and basic print and book conventions.

💬 Comprehension & oral language

Children are assessed on identifying characters and key events, sequencing and retelling stories, using vocabulary, answering questions, constructing sentences, and expressing their ideas.

📋 NCF competency rubrics

NCF-aligned competencies and learning outcomes are recorded monthly using age-appropriate progression levels to document individual development across key reading skills.

✅ Teacher moderation

Teacher observations and judgements are moderated to ensure consistency, using evidence from classroom activities, oral responses, reading behaviours, and recorded work.

🚀 Ongoing progress monitoring

Assessment identifies each child's strengths, areas for support, and next learning steps, combining reading data with teacher evidence to guide targeted instruction.

🌐 External Best-Practice Resources

Research-based references for teachers

🔤 Reading Rockets

Free, research-backed articles and videos on systematic phonics instruction and building phonemic awareness — the foundation skills behind our KG1 sound work.

📖 National Center on Improving Literacy

A practice guide on building K–3 reading comprehension through guided discussion and text structure — directly supports our KG2 Guided Reading approach.

🏛️ What Works Clearinghouse (IES)

The federal Foundational Skills practice guide for K–3 — the evidence base behind decodable-text and explicit-decoding routines.

💡 Consensus across these sources: phonemic awareness and systematic phonics come first, comprehension strategies are taught explicitly (not left to chance), and daily read-alouds with rich discussion build vocabulary alongside decoding — all already reflected in our Reading Plan and Strategies pages.
