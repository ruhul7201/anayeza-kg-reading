3 · Assessment & Data Moderation

Baseline data + continuous teacher assessment

Baseline reading level is established through Rosen LevelUp (Lexile score). Students are grouped into five reading bands — Emerging, Progressing, Proficient, Advanced, Exceeding — using the school's KG1 / KG2 banding keys. This is combined with continuous teacher-led guided-reading assessment against a structured rubric (phonics, blending, fluency, accuracy, comprehension, vocabulary), scored on its own five-point band (Beginner to Exemplary).

3.1 · Baseline reading bands

Rosen LevelUp (Lexile) — KG1 (n=96) vs KG2 (n=142), from the KG LevelUp Assessment Report. Values are % of students.

KG1 KG2
KG1 is weighted toward Progressing (38%), consistent with a younger cohort still building foundational decoding. KG2 shifts toward Proficient and above (63% combined), reflecting a full extra year of structured guided reading.

3.2 · Continuous assessment — KG2 Guided Reading Rubric

Average rubric score by criterion, latest cycle (June Week 4, n=143). Scale 1 = Beginner to 5 = Exemplary. Nine consecutive weekly cycles recorded, April Week 3 – June Week 4.

Phonics is the clear strength (3.91, above the Proficient benchmark of 3.0), with Blending (2.89) close behind. Vocabulary (1.83) and Accuracy (2.01) are furthest below benchmark and are the priority focus of the Section 4 intervention strategies.

3.3 · Data moderation process

Routine data checks flagged inconsistencies between some students' LevelUp Lexile placement and their observed in-class reading. A teacher-administered Mini Reading Test was given to the affected students and compared against their baseline Lexile placement.

242
Students on the KG1 + KG2 roster
36
Given a Mini Reading Test (14.9% of the 241 assessed)
58%
Of those re-tested were re-grouped to a more reliable level

Moderation outcome for the 36 students given a Mini Reading Test.

Where the Mini Reading Test confirmed the LevelUp placement, the group was left unchanged (15 students). Where it did not, the student was moved to match the teacher-administered result — 9 up a group, 12 down. The largest single discrepancy: a KG2A student LevelUp had placed at Level 1 (Emerging) who read on-or-above grade level on the Mini Test — a four-band jump. That case, with three related KG2A cases, was flagged for re-testing.

4 · Action Plan & Intervention Strategies

Targeted support at three levels

Groupings confirmed through the Section 3 moderation process, together with criterion-level rubric data, are used to target support.

Higher achievers
  • Advanced ORT levels & pleasure reading books
  • Focus on fluency, expression & comprehension depth
  • Leadership roles during reading sessions
Middle group
  • Reinforcement of phonics & blending
  • Guided reading with structured support
  • Regular comprehension practice
Lower achievers
  • Small-group intervention sessions
  • Pull-out support during Life Skills sessions
  • Phonics, decoding & sight words
  • Repeated reading for fluency

🎯 Priority focus areas — from rubric data

School-wide, KG2 rubric scores are strongest in Phonics (3.91) and Blending (2.89) but below the Proficient benchmark in Vocabulary (1.83), Accuracy (2.01), Comprehension (2.22) and Fluency (2.48). Small-group intervention time is prioritised toward Vocabulary and Accuracy — the two furthest below benchmark — while Phonics moves from taught instruction to independent application and self-correction for most students.

Students in the Emerging and Progressing bands — 48% of KG1 and 33% of KG2 — are the primary population for the pull-out and small-group strategies above. Moderation (3.3) ensures this targeting is based on verified, not just system-reported, reading levels. Differentiation ensures all learners progress at their appropriate level.

Teaching & Learning Strategies

Fun, Phonics & Reading Activities

Young children learn to read best when reading feels meaningful, active and fun. These experiences build phonics, vocabulary, comprehension, confidence and a love of books.

All grades · Pre-reading

🖼️ Picture Walk

Learners preview the pictures before reading to predict the story, activate prior knowledge and build vocabulary. In KG2 this stretches into higher-order thinking questions.

  • “Look at the cover — what do you see? What might happen?”
  • Doubles as a formative assessment tool for teachers
  • Pre-KG visits the Reading Room twice a week for Picture Walk + Read Aloud

KG1 · Phonics

🔤 Blend & Read

Phonics in action — learners blend phonemes into words and segment words back into sounds, moving from CVC words to simple sentences.

  • This week: Blend n Read — Story of Pip (slides + printable)

  • Reading stations with a poster-guided rotation

Teacher-supported reading in levelled groups using Oxford Reading Tree books. Students meet Biff, Chip, Kipper and Floppy while building fluency and comprehension.

  • Comprehension talk: characters, setting, fiction vs non-fiction
  • Groups are formed from Lexile levels and refreshed each term

📐 How We Measure Reading

Multiple sources of evidence · recorded throughout the year

Reading progress is assessed through a combination of standardised reading measures, teacher observation, oral reading, and competency-based assessment to provide a well-rounded picture of each child's development.

📊 Rosen LevelUp baseline & Lexile tracking

Children's reading ability is established through a Rosen LevelUp baseline, with Lexile measures used where appropriate to monitor reading growth and guide suitable reading levels and texts.

👀 Teacher observation & oral reading

Teachers regularly observe children during reading, storytelling, phonics activities, and individual oral reading to assess accuracy, fluency, vocabulary, comprehension, confidence, and expression.

🔤 Early literacy & phonological skills

Assessment includes recognition of sounds, beginning/middle/ending sounds, blending and segmenting, familiar word recognition, and basic print and book conventions.

💬 Comprehension & oral language

Children are assessed on identifying characters and key events, sequencing and retelling stories, using vocabulary, answering questions, constructing sentences, and expressing their ideas.

📋 NCF competency rubrics

NCF-aligned competencies and learning outcomes are recorded monthly using age-appropriate progression levels to document individual development across key reading skills.

✅ Teacher moderation

Teacher observations and judgements are moderated to ensure consistency, using evidence from classroom activities, oral responses, reading behaviours, and recorded work.

🚀 Ongoing progress monitoring

Assessment identifies each child's strengths, areas for support, and next learning steps, combining reading data with teacher evidence to guide targeted instruction.
